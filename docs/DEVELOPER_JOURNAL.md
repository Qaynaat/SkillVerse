## Reflection

SkillVerse started as a learning companion.

During development, I realised the real problem wasn't a lack of courses.

It was a lack of guidance.

That discovery completely changed the vision of SkillVerse.

Today, SkillVerse is becoming a Career Navigation Platform rather than another learning platform.

This decision shaped every future feature.

# Looking Forward

The next stage of SkillVerse focuses on making Byte more intelligent.

Instead of simply answering questions, Byte will understand students, explain careers, build personalised roadmaps, and guide them throughout their journey.

The goal isn't to build another chatbot.

The goal is to build the mentor I wish I had.
